How the decoder works

  1. Exact match: we check the number against our dictionary of known codes (520, 1314, 666…).
  2. Segmentation: longer numbers are split into known codes and single digits, then read as a phrase.
  3. Luck score (0–100): digit weights (8, 6, 9 up; 4 down), plus patterns such as repeats, runs, endings, and combos like 14 or 250.
  4. Digit sum & root: for numerology fans.

Lucky phone number checker

What matters in a phone number

  • The last 4 digits carry the most weight. People say them out loud most often.
  • Avoid 4 at the end, and combos like 14 (要死), 74 (气死) and 250.
  • Premium patterns: AAAA, AABB, ABAB, 168, 518, 888, 1688.
  • In China, 1 is read as “yāo” in phone numbers, so 1 sounds like 要 “want”.

License plate checker

Plate luck, in short

We only score the digits; letters are ignored. Across Chinese-speaking regions, plates with repeated 8s, 6s and 9s are in high demand, and plates with 4 are avoided.

Plate rules and auction systems vary by region. Check with your local transport authority before bidding.

Price optimizer

Why price endings matter

In markets influenced by Chinese culture, prices ending in 8, 88, 68 or 99 signal prosperity and good wishes. Endings in 4 and totals like 250 send the wrong message. The optimizer suggests the nearest culturally positive prices above and below yours.

About date luck

We score a date by the numbers in it (for example 8/8, 6/6, 9/9, 5/20), minus days with 4, 14 and 24. Traditional almanac (黄历) selection also uses the lunar calendar and your birth details. For big events, ask a qualified practitioner, or request a free report.
