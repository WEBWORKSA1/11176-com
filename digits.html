The Meaning of Every Digit, 0–9

Each digit's Chinese character, pronunciation, sound-alike words, and why it's loved or avoided.

Why numbers carry meaning in Chinese

Mandarin has a small set of syllables and many homophones. So a number's sound can suggest a word. Businesses pay attention to this, and so do families and couples:

  • Buildings often skip the 4th, 14th and 24th floors.
  • Phone numbers and plates full of 8s sell at a premium. Wikipedia cites a Chengdu number, +86 28 8888 8888, that sold for about US$280,000 in 2003.
  • Big events choose lucky dates. The Beijing Olympics opened on 08/08/2008 at 8:08:08 pm.
  • Online, digits make up a fast slang: 520 (I love you), 666 (awesome), 88 (bye-bye).

Cantonese readings sometimes differ. For example, 3 sounds like 生 (life) and 9 like 狗 (dog) in some jokes. Where the regional reading matters, our tools point it out.
