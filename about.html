About 11176

A friendly, independent guide to the meaning of numbers in Chinese culture.

Our mission

Numbers shape real decisions across the Chinese-speaking world: which phone number to buy, which floor to live on, what to charge, when to marry. 11176 makes this knowledge clear, searchable and fun for everyone, in English first and in more languages over time.

What we do

  • Free tools that decode and score any number.
  • A growing dictionary of number slang and culture codes.
  • Free personal reports and business audits.
  • Videos, contests and a community of number-lovers.

Editorial standards

Our meanings are drawn from widely documented homophones, popular internet usage and published cultural references. Readings of numbers without an established meaning, including our own name, are clearly labeled as interpretations. Luck scores are rules-based and transparent. We correct errors quickly — report one here.

How we're funded

Advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, optional referral partnerships, and reader support. Sponsored content is always labeled.
