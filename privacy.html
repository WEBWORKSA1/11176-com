Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains how 11176.com (“we”) collects and uses information.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit in forms (name, email, numbers, messages, optional phone or birth year). We use it only to answer you, send your report, run contests, or process your support pledge.
  • Numbers you type into tools are processed in your browser and are not sent to us, unless you submit a form.
  • Cookies and similar technologies for preferences (theme, consent), analytics (Google Analytics, if enabled) and advertising (Google AdSense), only after you accept.

Form processing

Forms are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form relay service (FormSubmit). Submissions pass through that service solely to reach us.

Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See also how Google uses data from partner sites.

Your choices & rights

You can choose “Essential only” in the cookie banner, clear cookies at any time, unsubscribe from emails, or ask us to access, correct or delete your data through the contact form. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA or Quebec Law 25.

Retention & security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose, and at most 24 months unless the law requires longer. We never sell personal information.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post updates on this page.
